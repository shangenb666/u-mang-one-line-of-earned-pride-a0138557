At sixty-one, after two failed attempts and a year of night classes, she pinned the results letter to the fridge, under her grandson's drawing, where the important things go.

The program writes that line to `entry.txt` in the output directory.
One line, at most 240 characters, judged in pairs on genuine earned
pride, a concrete scene, landing in one read, and freshness.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
